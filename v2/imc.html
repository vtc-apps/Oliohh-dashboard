Training of process matrix

1-Store identification & Store visit (CCR)

Store Identification:

  • Search for new potential stores in the assigned area.
  • Verify store details and business potential.
  • Collect complete store's information as per policy (Geo Tag Location, GST no, Mobile, Address).
  • Share details to system team to add in software.

Store Visit (CCR- Call Compliance Report):

  • Visit stores as per assigned route plan.
  • Check stock availability and discuss product requirements.
  • Resolve customer concerns and handle objections.
  • Update visit status in the system as per CCR norms.

2-Order Booking (BCP) & Order transfer to Billing

Order Booking (BCP-Basic Call Procedure):

  • Follow the BCP Step for order taking.
  • Verify stock availability.
  • Enter orders correctly in the Bizom application only.
  • Confirm order details and delivery date before submission.

Order Transfer to Billing:

  • Verify booked orders.
  • Transfer orders to billing on time to branch server via application.
  • Coordinate with billing and logistics teams if changes required.

3-Daily review & Monthly Review

Daily Review:

  • Review daily sales and visit target performance.
  • Check all the stores CCR and pending activities.
  • Discuss challenges, identify issues and action plans.
  • Next Day PFD target plan.
  • Fill the review book/Tracker summary as per company policy.

Monthly Review:

  • Monthly review scorecard format will set as per tracking mechanism.
  • Measure overall focus area and analyze monthly sales performance.
  • Review target achievement and market opportunities.

1 - HR-Attendance & Hiring

Hiring:-

  • Ensures the right manpower is available for business growth.
  • Identify manpower requirements.
  • Source and screen candidates and Conduct interviews as per hiring process and selection.
  • Complete joining formalities.

Attendance Management:-

  • Ensures employee presence and discipline.
  • All the HR activities in HR MS software not manual innervations.
  • Monitor daily attendance.
  • Track market working hours and GPS records(CCR).
  • Follow up on missing attendance.
  • Update attendance records accurately.
  • Resolve attendance discrepancies.

2 - HR-OC Training & Monthly Capability Training

OC Training:-

  • OC Training is an initial training program provided to all new employees at the time of joining. The training duration is a minimum of 3 days. During this session, all new joiners are introduced to the company's Rules & Regulations (R&R), policies, processes, organizational structure, code of conduct, and work culture.
  • Plan OC training schedules..
  • Conduct department-wise process training..
  • Track training completion.

Monthly Capability Training:-

  • Identify training needs..
  • Conduct monthly capability sessions..
  • Evaluate employee participation.
  • Monitor improvement actions.

DRCP Process & Order Collection

DRCP Process (Daily coverage route Plan) :

  • Prepare and maintain customers master data with Geo Tag Locations.
  • Send Dump data to Stack Box (Outsource company) to make route according to shared norms( Like One area all sellers , distance maintain , no store in beat fixed)
  • Check all the received DRCP routes and get resolve error and send DRCP to sales manager before the uploading in system.
  • Final DRCP Upload in system and rollout to resale team.

Order Collection in system:-

  • Collect market orders from the sellers app.
  • Verify customer and product details.
  • Check for missing or pending orders.
  • Check company policy before the invoice generation (Like- Double bill policy , Contact details
  • Coordinate with the sales team if required and generate Order for stock delivery

DCR / Reporting / Software

System DCR Report & Other Reporting:

  • Prepare DCR and MIS reports.
  • Validate report accuracy.
  • Share reports within timelines.
  • Follow up on report discrepancies.

Software Management:-

  • Manage software access and user creation..
  • Ensures smooth business operations.
  • Supports all departments through system availability.
  • Coordinate software updates and changes.
  • Improves efficiency and data accuracy.

1-Customer Credit Management

Customer Credit Management Guidelines:

  • Credit will be given as per Channel wise AR Norm policy.
  • Monitor customer credit limits and track outstanding payments regularly.
  • Coordinate with the sales team for overdue cases and ensure healthy cash flow.
  • Escalate customers exceeding credit limits / overdue payment for reduced financial risk.

2-Expense Management

Expense Management Guidelines:

  • Review branch expenses regularly.
  • Verify expenses against approved budgets.
  • Ensure proper approvals before payments.
  • Identify unnecessary expenses.
  • Share monthly expense analysis.
  • Helps control company costs.

3-GST, Accounting & Ledgers

GST & Accounting:

  • Ensures compliance with government regulations.
  • Verify accounting entries and ensure proper GST compliance.
  • Reconcile sales, purchases, and tax records.
  • Maintain accounting documents and support GST filing and audit requirements.

Ledger Management (Key Customers):

  • Maintain customer ledgers regularly.
  • Reconcile balances with customers.
  • Monitor debit and credit transactions.
  • Resolve ledger mismatches promptly.
  • Support collection and outstanding tracking to map balances accurately.

Stock Inward & Delivery

Purchase Stock Inward:

  • Verify shipment documents.
  • Check product quantity and condition..
  • Report shortages, damages, or mismatches.
  • Send Shipment invoice to system team for GRN (Stock in system)
  • Store products in the designated location.
  • Always verify before accepting stock

Delivery:-

  • Deliver products as per invoice.
  • Follow the planned delivery route.
  • Ensure products reach customers safely..
  • Coordinate with customers when any issues.
  • Always verify before delivering stock.

MDC & SRN (Return)

MDC (Market Delivery Cost):

  • Follow approved transportation methods.
  • Record delivery expenses accurately.
  • Monitor route efficiency.
  • Helps control delivery expenses.
  • Submit cost records supporting.

SRN (Return):-

  • Ensures proper handling of returned products.
  • Verify returned products and return reasons.
  • Check past purchase from VTC history of returned product
  • Take the approval and enter in system
  • Adjust proper Credit note in system

Damage, Proof & Purchase

Damage (Expiry):

  • Identify damaged or expired stock. (As per damage expired policy)
  • Segregate affected products.
  • Check past purchase from VTC history of returned product

Delivery Proof (POD):-

  • Delivered stock to customer as per the order invoice
  • Collect customer signature/stamp if AR Customers otherwise collect cash.
  • Verify delivery details.
  • Submit POD invoice on time at branch cashier

Purchase /Shipment Process :-

  • Place the Order to vender according to requirement and trend with time line.
  • Ensures stock availability as per lead day of order shipment.
  • Coordinate purchase shipments to timely received.

VTC Food Division - Follow the Process Matrix

Resale

Identify new stores as per guidelines, Visit assigned stores as per route plan, follow BCP steps for order booking, transfer orders on time, and participate in daily and monthly reviews. Ensure all market activities are updated in the system.

System

DRCP preparation, accurate invoicing, Ensure timely reporting, software management, and issue resolution. Support all departments and close system requirements within defined timelines.

Logistics

Follow Inventory management, ensure timely delivery, maintain POD records, manage returns and damages as per policy, and control delivery costs effectively.

Finance

Monitor collections, outstanding payments, customer credit limits, expenses, and operational activities. Ensure timely reconciliation and adherence to financial controls.

HR

Follow hiring, onboarding, attendance, and training processes. Ensure employees are trained, qualified, and compliant with company policies and procedures.

Operations

Follow approved business expansion and Sub-Distributor (Sub-D) processes. Ensure implementation as per company norms and monitor operational effectiveness.

Common process guidelines (for all departments)

  1. Understand the SOP and department responsibilities.
  2. Follow the approved process without deviation.
  3. Update all activities in the system on time.
  4. Escalate issues through the proper channel.
  5. Maintain required records and documentation.
  6. Participate in daily, weekly, and monthly reviews.
  7. Focus on accuracy, compliance, and continuous improvement.
“"Understand the process, follow the SOP, update activities on time, maintain proper records, and escalate issues through the defined channel. Consistent process adherence ensures operational efficiency, compliance, and business growth."”
