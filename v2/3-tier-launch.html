Project owner Mr. SunilLast updated March 2026
  1. Tier 1

    Awareness & teasers

    • Digital “Mystery” Campaign for Oli-Ohh Pasta.
    • Social Media influencer collaborations (Food Bloggers).
    • Pre-launch buzz in major Mandis and Retail Hubs.
    • Email teasers to existing VTC Group distributors.
  2. Tier 2This week’s focus

    The soft launch

    • Direct distribution to Top 50 Retailers in the Roadmap.
    • Quick Commerce listing (Blinkit/Zepto) trial runs.
    • Launch Ceremony for internal stakeholders and VIP partners.
    • Margin structure finalization for Railway Channels.
  3. Tier 3

    Aggressive scale-up

    • Full E-Commerce availability (Amazon/Flipkart).
    • Mandi Activation: On-ground stalls and sampling events.
    • Targeting “100 Retailors Roadmap” completion.
    • BTL Marketing: Hoardings and Railway station branding.
Strategist notes. This plan focuses on maintaining high margins while ensuring the “Oli-Ohh” brand is perceived as a premium yet accessible choice in the pasta segment. Focus for this week is Tier 2 execution.
